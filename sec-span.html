    9.

    Let \(A = \left[{\begin{matrix}4 \amp -4 \cr 6 \amp -6 \cr \end{matrix}}\right]\text{.}\)
    🔗
    We want to determine if the columns of \(A\) span \({\mathbb R}^2\text{.}\)
    🔗

    🔗
    • The columns of \(A\) do span \({\mathbb R}^2\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
