    8.

    Express the following vector equation as a system of linear equations.
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} x_1 \left[\begin{matrix} 1 \cr 7 \cr \end{matrix}\right]+ x_2 \left[\begin{matrix} -1 \cr 1 \cr \end{matrix}\right]= \left[\begin{matrix} -1 \cr -4 \cr \end{matrix}\right] \end{equation*}
    🔗
    (Keep the equations in order.)
    🔗
    \(x_1 +\) \(x_2 =\) .
    🔗
    \(x_1 +\) \(x_2 =\) .
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{equation*} 1 x_1 - 1 x_2 = -1 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} 7 x_1+ 1 x_2 = -4 \end{equation*}
